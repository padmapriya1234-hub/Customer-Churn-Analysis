# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn using the Telco Customer Churn dataset.

The analysis explores customer demographics, services, contracts, billing information, tenure, payment methods, and customer status to understand patterns related to customer churn.

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about 7,043 customers.

- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Churn Rate: 26.54%

## Analysis Performed

The following factors were analyzed:

- Customer churn distribution
- Churn by gender
- Churn by contract type
- Churn by internet service
- Monthly charges and churn
- Tenure and churn
- Total revenue and churn
- Payment method and churn
- Customer status and churn
- Churn reasons

## Key Findings

- The overall customer churn rate is 26.54%.
- Churn rates were relatively similar between male and female customers.
- Contract type showed differences in customer churn.
- Monthly charges and tenure were analyzed in relation to churn.
- Competitor-related factors were among the frequently reported churn reasons.

## Conclusion

This analysis provides an overview of customer churn patterns in the Telco dataset. The findings can help identify areas for further investigation, such as customer retention, competitive offers, service and support experience, and pricing.
